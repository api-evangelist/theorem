# Theorem

> Theorem builds machine-learning-powered credit underwriting infrastructure for consumer debt markets. Since 2014 it has priced and purchased billions of dollars in loan volume, providing instant, API-delivered credit assessments to lending originators and packaging loans into credit products for capital-market investors.

Theorem operates an institutional-grade underwriting platform. Its API is a private, partner-onboarded B2B integration (there is no public developer portal, published OpenAPI specification, or self-service sign-up); technical integration is arranged through the partnerships team.

## Products
- [Theorem Loan Score (TLS)](https://www.theoremlp.com/our-technology): Machine-learning evaluation of the default risk of a loan, analyzed down to individual cashflows and the expected value of each payment.
- [Custom Loan Integration Programs (CLIP)](https://www.theoremlp.com/our-technology): A decision engine that plugs into a partner's existing underwriting flow — from calling the API for instant results to deeper bespoke integrations.
- [Securitization as a Service (SaaS)](https://www.theoremlp.com/our-technology): Packages loans into credit products and connects originators to capital-market investors.
